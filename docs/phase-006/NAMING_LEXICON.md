## Naming pattern rules

Buildings: concrete civic/industrial/network nouns, usually one to three words: `Granary`, `Relay Post`, `Charter Vault`. Avoid ornamental fantasy prefixes unless they encode a real function.

Enemies: role/silhouette/behavior first. Names should help forecast behavior rather than conceal it.

Upgrades: short relationship or behavior phrases. Prefer “Linked Stores” over “Bounty of the Eternal Harvest.”

Events: civic problems or compromises, not lore chapter titles.

Bosses: exceptional proper names may be poetic because they are few and heavily telegraphed. Existing working names remain **The Mute Colossus**, **The Pale Cartographer**, **The Crownless Choir**.

Internal IDs are permanent technical identity. Display names may change through localization or approved writing review without save migration.
