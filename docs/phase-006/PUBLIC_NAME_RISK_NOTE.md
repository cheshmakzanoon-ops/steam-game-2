# Phase 006 — Public-name risk note

**Current working title:** Charterwake: The Last Relay  
**Status:** AMBER / PROVISIONAL  
**Review date:** 2026-09-22

This is production risk management, not legal advice or trademark clearance.

## Current screen

A fresh exact-name public-web screen on 2026-09-22 did not surface an obvious exact videogame/software use for **Charterwake**. Targeted search-engine queries for exact-name results on Steam and itch.io likewise returned no result in the searched index.

That is only a preliminary collision screen. Search engines can miss:
- pending/unindexed marks;
- similar-sounding marks;
- common-law use;
- company/product use outside game storefronts;
- relevant regional registrations.

Therefore the name remains **AMBER**, not GREEN.

## Historical reason for replacement

The earlier working title **Signalhold: The Last Relay** is retired. Phase 002 found UK application **UK00004235663** for the identical word SIGNALHOLD in software-related classes. Historical evidence remains in Phase 002 rather than being erased.

## Required formal search before public title lock

Before Steam-page publication, final logo commission, trademark filing or major paid title-dependent marketing, update and retain searches through:

- CIPO Canadian Trademarks Database;
- USPTO Trademark Search;
- UK IPO;
- EUIPO / TMview;
- WIPO Global Brand Database;
- Steam, itch.io, GOG, Epic and relevant console/mobile storefronts;
- general web/company/domain/social/common-law sources.

Exact and confusingly similar variants must be reviewed; no-result exact search is not enough.

## Public-use rule

Allowed now:
- repository/internal project name;
- private prototype labels;
- internal planning;
- replaceable draft word-shape exploration.

Not yet treated as cleared:
- public Steam store identity;
- paid final capsule/logo dependent on the wordmark;
- merchandise;
- trademark claim or ® symbol;
- claim that the name is legally safe.

## Replacement procedure

If Charterwake becomes unacceptable:

1. freeze title-dependent public spending;
2. record the conflicting evidence and risk disposition;
3. choose replacement candidates under the same lexicon/originality rules;
4. run exact/fuzzy/official/common-law screens;
5. project owner selects the new provisional name;
6. update README, Phase 002/006 naming docs and store/marketing metadata;
7. preserve internal stable content/save IDs;
8. regenerate wordmark/key-art text layers from source;
9. rerun localization/search/links;
10. keep the old-name decision record for provenance.

No gameplay rule, save schema or content ID may require migration merely because the marketing title changes.

## Phase 006 validation conclusion

**Charterwake is acceptable as the internal/provisional working title for continued preproduction. It is not certified as the final public trademark.**
