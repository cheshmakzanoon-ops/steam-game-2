# Phase 006 — Provisional logo brief

**Title:** Charterwake: The Last Relay  
**Status:** WORD-SHAPE / BRAND BRIEF ONLY — NOT FINAL ART  
**Public-name status:** provisional

Phase 006 does not authorize final logo art. This brief defines constraints so later logo work supports the game's material language and remains replaceable if the public title changes.

## Primary word shape

**CHARTERWAKE** is the dominant shape.

Requirements:
- readable at Steam capsule thumbnail scale;
- broad, stable horizontal silhouette rather than a tall fantasy crest;
- distinguishable C / H / A / R / T / E / R / W / A / K / E at small size;
- no compressed blackletter that turns into texture;
- no illegible distressed treatment;
- enough internal negative space to survive paper/ink effects;
- avoid a crown icon as the dominant mark, because the player is a Warden and the project must not visually resemble “king” reference titles;
- avoid an eye/gaze icon or rectangular viewing frame;
- avoid literal electrical bolt/circuit-board clichés.

## Subtitle

**THE LAST RELAY** is secondary.

- may sit below or beside Charterwake;
- must remain readable when included;
- may be omitted from the smallest in-game mark after full-title establishment;
- must not become a separate badge resembling DLC.

## Material treatment

Preferred exploration:
- printed charcoal edge;
- restrained desaturated-gold signal thread;
- slight paper emboss/deboss;
- one deliberate misregistration/accent layer;
- torn/stitched line motif only if legibility survives.

Avoid:
- chrome/3D esports bevels;
- neon cyberpunk circuitry;
- photoreal medieval metal;
- generic shield/crest framing;
- competitor palettes or silhouettes.

## Functional mark idea

If a symbol is needed, explore a **broken charter line rejoined through one relay knot**. It must remain abstract enough not to resemble a real-world heraldic mark, religious mark, electrical schematic symbol or competitor icon.

## Layout variants reserved

1. horizontal full title;
2. stacked full title;
3. Charterwake wordmark alone;
4. small relay-knot mark only after the wordmark is established.

These are layout variants, not four separate logo identities.

## Accessibility and production

- test grayscale and high-contrast readability;
- do not depend on color for title recognition;
- preserve vector/source layers;
- retain source/license records for any font or texture;
- do not purchase/commission title-dependent final art until the naming-validation gate permits it;
- create final marketing exports only from approved source.

## Replacement resilience

No logo geometry may be embedded as an authoritative gameplay icon. If the public title changes, the game must remain buildable with a neutral internal project ID and replacement wordmark.
