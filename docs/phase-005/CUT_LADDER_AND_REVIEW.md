# Phase 005 — Cut ladder and monthly scope review

Status: planning control, not authorization to cut a required feature. Project owner is the final approver.

## Ranked cut ladder

| Rank | ID | Action | Trigger | Protected boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CUT-01 | Stop unapproved modes, biomes and online features | Any proposed launch expansion | Reject scope creep; no baseline deliverable removed. |
| 2 | CUT-02 | Reduce decorative props and marketing compositions | Art effort exceeds allocation | Functional readability remains protected. |
| 3 | CUT-03 | Replace non-functional unique animation with shared masks/transforms | Frame/render throughput fails | Never remove powered, damaged, danger or focus readability. |
| 4 | CUT-04 | Reduce optional codex/event prose, not systemic choices | Text/localization ceiling threatened | Preserve rules, warnings, onboarding and accessibility text. |
| 5 | CUT-05 | Use five adaptive pieces rather than seven | Audio allowance threatened | Preserve Command/threat cues and mix controls. |
| 6 | CUT-06 | Defer optional target languages before announcement | Cash/localization capacity fails | English + localization-ready architecture remain. |
| 7 | CUT-07 | Propose redundant launch-content reductions/substitutions | Earlier cuts insufficient | Requires explicit owner decision and revised coverage. |
| 8 | CUT-08 | Move a public milestone or stop/re-scope | Quality red, reserve exhausted, or >18-month forecast | Never buy schedule by dropping saves, determinism, licensing or accessibility. |

## Non-negotiables

Never trade away the 9×9 one-board identity, scarce Command, transparent forecasts, deterministic simulation, safe saves/recovery, fair-loss explanation, offline play, source/license traceability or accessibility/input parity. Four doctrines, three bosses and the 15-Watch structure remain the approved baseline unless separately changed through formal change control.

## Monthly review form

Record, without overwriting prior reviews:

- review ID/date, model version and baseline commit;
- reviewer/product approver;
- completed gates and supporting commits;
- accepted/forecast final counts by canonical category;
- visual sets/color frames/render passes;
- source words/languages/translation exposure;
- audio cues/variants/music/stems;
- install/process/texture measurements with hardware/build/method;
- actual labor, remaining estimate and final forecast;
- actual paid costs, commitments and remaining allowance;
- living-cost/available-cash input or explicit “not provided”;
- current quality risks with owner/evidence/action;
- candidate cuts and non-overlapping savings;
- decision: KEEP / CUT-AND-REFORECAST / STOP-PUBLIC-MILESTONE / ESCALATE;
- owner approval and next due gate.

## Trigger logic

- Any content/frame/word/cue/size ceiling exceeded: **RED**; reject additions until replacement or approved baseline change.
- Forecast using over 80% of productive prelaunch capacity: **AMBER**; reserve below 20%.
- Forecast beyond full capacity, quality-gate failure or >18-month production forecast: **RED**; stop public commitments.
- Cash forecast above selected scenario ceiling: **RED** pending funding/scope disposition.
- Personal runway not supplied: **UNSET**, never GREEN.
- Missing rights, corrupted saves, preview/simulation contradiction or inaccessible required input: **RED** regardless of schedule/cash.

## Boundary and recovery cases

Exactly 400 color frames or 12,000 source words is legal; the next unit fails. Exactly 20% reserve is legal; less triggers review. Negative/fractional counts, hidden extra content, malformed data, untracked variants or missing private inputs treated as zero are invalid.

Recovery means retain the accepted revision, correct the proposed source or obtain explicit scope-change approval, rebuild/revalidate, then review again.
